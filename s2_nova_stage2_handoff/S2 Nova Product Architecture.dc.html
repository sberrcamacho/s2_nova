S2 Nova: multiplatform product architecture

Stage 1 · Conceptual architecture before Android and Web interface design · Revised 23 Sep 2026 against the repository (sberrcamacho/s2_nova@main)

Convention: this document is in English. Text in quotes (e.g. "Nuevo movimiento") is Spanish UI copy and must be implemented verbatim.

0. Premises to adjust

Five points in the brief cause conflicts if taken literally.

  1. "Personal and business". Business finance means invoicing, taxes, multiple users with permissions and cost centers. That is a different product. Decision: business finance is out of scope. S2 Nova is personal finance only.
  2. Full parity for barcode scanning. Scanning depends on the phone camera. What is shared is the result (a purchase with line items), not the capture method. Web can view and edit a purchase but cannot scan.
  3. Budget periods and renewal. The backend supports MONTHLY only (BudgetPeriod enum), with an implicit reset each month. Decision: the MVP keeps monthly budgets as implemented. Section 9 lists other periods as future extensions.
  4. Wallets, recurring items and loans. They are not in the brief's core list, but all three are implemented in the backend and on Android. Decision: wallets, Programados and Préstamos are MVP. An earlier draft of this document moved loans to phase 2; that is withdrawn.
  5. COP/USD currency. Today it is a format switch. Real multi-currency needs exchange rates and historical conversion. For the MVP it is a display currency.

1. Value proposition

Know how much money you have, where it goes and whether you will meet your plans: log in seconds from your phone, analyze in depth from your computer.

The product rests on one habit, logging transactions, and two questions that habit answers: "how am I doing this month?" (budgets) and "will I get there?" (goals). Everything else serves those two questions or is out.

2. Shared mental model

A user should be able to explain S2 Nova in one sentence: everything starts with a transaction. A transaction leaves or enters a wallet, has a category, and that category feeds a budget. Goals receive contributions, which are also transactions.

WHERE FROM
Wallet
"Billetera"
WHAT HAPPENED
Transaction
"Movimiento": expense, income or transfer
ON WHAT
Category
"Categoría"
HOW MUCH I CAN
Budget
"Presupuesto": limit per period
SAVING FOR
Goal
"Meta": target with contributions

Rules both platforms follow without exception:

  • Total balance is the sum of wallet balances. No other balance exists.
  • A budget holds no money: it measures spending in its categories within its period.
  • A goal contribution is a transaction out of a wallet. Withdrawing from a goal returns money to a wallet.
  • A scanned purchase is an expense with attached line items, not a separate entity.
  • Budgets, goals and loans are the three kinds of Plan: amounts the user tracks over time against a target.
  • A loan is a transaction with a counterparty. Lending takes money out of a wallet; each "abono" received brings money back into a wallet (the same one or another). Borrowing works in reverse.

Cross-platform consistency

  • The backend is the only source of truth. Neither client keeps data the other can't see.
  • Examples that must hold: a loan created on Android appears on Web; an abono made on Web is visible on Android; deleting a transaction on Web updates the affected budget, goal and wallet, and Android shows the change.
  • Propagation, as implemented (ARCHITECTURE.md §10): every mutation refetches its affected lists on the client that made it. The other client picks up the change when it refetches: Android on screen focus and pull-to-refresh, Web on route change and window focus. There is no push or real-time channel today. "Immediately" therefore means "on the next screen visit". Real-time would need SSE, which is out of scope for the MVP.
  • No financial capability is exclusive to one platform. Web-only items are depth (more charts, comparisons, history per item) and CSV. Android-only items are hardware (camera) and mobile affordances (push, auto-lock).

6. Android navigation

Bottom bar: Inicio · Movimientos · [+] · Planes · Reportes. Profile opens from the header avatar.

  • Transactions moves from a stacked screen to a tab: after logging, checking history is the most frequent action.
  • Profile leaves the bar because it is rarely visited; a header avatar is a familiar pattern.
  • The center button keeps two actions: "Nuevo movimiento" and "Escanear compra". Type (expense, income, transfer) is chosen inside the form.
  • Wallets open from the wallet chip on the Home balance card and from Profile.

8. Primary user flows

F1. Log an expense (Android)

[+] › "Nuevo movimiento" › amount › description (category is suggested: "mercado" → Alimentación) › preselected wallet › "Guardar". Target: under 10 seconds, at most three taps after typing the amount.

F2. Log a purchase by barcode (Android)

[+] › "Escanear compra" › camera reads each product › lookup order: (1) S2 Nova's global catalog (GET /products/:barcode); (2) Open Food Facts, name only; (3) no match: the user types name, category and price, and POST /products registers it for everyone › review list and total › wallet › "Guardar". Result: one expense linked to its product.

F3. Review and correct the month (Web)

Movimientos › filter by period and category › select several rows › recategorize or delete › affected budgets recalculate, and the change appears on Android on next sync.

F4. Create a budget (both)

Planes › "Nuevo presupuesto" › name › category (suggested from the name) › monthly limit › "Guardar". Alerts at 90%.

F5. Create a goal and contribute (both)

Planes › Metas › "Nueva meta" › target, date, source wallet. Contribute: goal card › "Aportar" › amount › deducted from the wallet. Web additionally shows progress over time and estimated date.

F6. Cross-platform continuity

The phone receives "Alimentación llegó al 90%". The user taps the notification and sees the budget. That evening they open Web, go to the same budget and review which transactions drove it there.

F8. Lend money and record abonos (both)

Planes › Préstamos › "Prestado" › "+ Registrar préstamo" › person, amount, wallet, optional due date › "Guardar": the amount leaves the wallet. Later: loan card › "Registrar abono" › amount (defaults to the outstanding balance), destination wallet › "Guardar": a repayment transaction enters that wallet; at zero outstanding the card shows "Saldado". Borrowing uses "Recibido" with directions reversed. Both platforms alert when a due date is near; Web adds the abono history per loan.

F7. Monthly analysis (Web)

Reportes › period › compare with previous › review categories with the largest change › export CSV.

13. MVP and secondary features

MVP

  • Sign-up, sign-in and cross-platform sync
  • Wallets with balance
  • Transactions: create, read, update, delete
  • History with search and filters
  • Manageable categories with keyword suggestion
  • Monthly budgets and 50/30/20 recommendation
  • Goals with contributions and withdrawals
  • Loans: lend, borrow, partial abonos, settle
  • Barcode purchase on Android
  • Home on both platforms
  • Web reports: trend, by category, month-over-month, plans
  • CSV export
  • Programados (scheduled transactions)
  • Profile, language, display currency, theme, sessions

Secondary

  • Budget carry-over
  • Alerts beyond budget thresholds
  • Saved filters and bulk editing
  • Multi-currency with conversion
  • PDF export
